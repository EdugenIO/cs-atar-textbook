## What changes for 2027?

Manual section-by-section comparison of the actual official web-rendered PDFs found rewritten rationale, aims and general capabilities, including Digital literacy terminology and five focus capabilities. Intercultural understanding and Personal and social capability are optional opportunities. Cybersecurity spelling and document layout/pagination also change.

No substantive change was found to the technical Unit 3/4 demands, school-assessment constraints/weightings or examination design. AI, cloud computing and machine-learning references in capability/context prose do not, by themselves, add compulsory technical unit content. General capabilities apply through the specified course content; teachers should use appropriate contexts and retain the technology process, communication, ethical reasoning and numerical demands already taught.

The retained Year 12 school-assessment categories are Project 40%, Theory 10%, Practical 10%, Examination 40%; maximum eight summative tasks, each at least 5%. The examined design remains ten minutes reading and three hours working, short answer 40% (20–30 questions) and extended answer 60% (4–6 questions). Actual school-issued assessments and annual examination instructions still require their own current checks.
